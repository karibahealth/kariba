After hours Nurse Line

What it is:

Primarily, we are offering peace of mind. We have a team of nurses armed with your individual medical history and advanced triage systems to help you make decisions about your health at times when our doctors arent available.

Without the personal touch and access to your accurate data we believe you are no better off than with a google search. We feel that your care should continue even after the work day has ended.

How it works

You will call our dedicated triage line and make a recording outlining your concerns. One of our nurses then gets assigned to the case and opens your records to begin formulating a plan. Armed with the information and your details the nurse now calls you back to help advise and guide you towards the best choices
